{% comment %}The kind a slot resolves to, at the slot mark's corner (spec 213). A fill-in and a choice say what they are in the mark's own glyph; what they will become is stated only in the words beside them. This badge carries it in the mark's language: the same glyphs the page already uses for the kind -- the note, the open book, the frame, the lines -- at the size the 20px seat leaves for them. Drawn on a 12-unit grid rather than the 24 the seat's own marks use, because a 24-unit glyph scaled into this 10px box strokes at about 0.75 device px — under a pixel. The book drops the cross it carries at full size: at this size it is a blob, and the two panels are what make a book.{% endcomment %}
