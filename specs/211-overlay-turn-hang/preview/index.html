What the slice changes

The continuation row is indented by exactly the marked row's column and gap — var(--speaker-col) plus .45em — so its first glyph lands on the words x. The voice class and its colour are untouched; the panel's type and motion are untouched. The plate hugs its widest row, so a continuation that is that row may widen the panel by the indent, inside the 90 % cap; the marked words and the continuation still share one x.

  • A screen with a mark — the continuation hangs at that screen's words column.
  • A screen with no mark — no column exists, so no indent is added.
  • A cue or ordinary line — empty voice, unchanged.
  • A wrapped continuation — every row stays at the column and inside the plate.
