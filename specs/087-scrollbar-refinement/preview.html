The steppers come from the standard properties, not from a missing button rule: scrollbar-width / scrollbar-color win over ::-webkit-scrollbar* in Chromium, so the native thin bar is drawn — and measured here it is 10px wide with an up-arrow glyph sitting above the thumb, while the custom bar is 5px with no glyph at all. Hiding ::-webkit-scrollbar-button alone cannot help, because the selector belongs to a pseudo-element the browser is not using. The resting thumb is deliberately quieter than a 2:1 indicator would be; the hover state stays clearly stronger.
