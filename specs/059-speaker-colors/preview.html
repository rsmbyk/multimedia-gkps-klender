Speaker colors

The screen faces the congregation. Their own lines stay the ordinary off-white, the text they read. A leader line is only a cue that the leader is speaking, so it is dimmer than that off-white and is not the line the eye settles on. A joined line is different: they do read it, and the color tells them it is theirs to say. It stays readable, and it stays quieter than the off-white. The gold line is the section header, unchanged.
