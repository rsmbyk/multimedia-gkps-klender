The corner, line by line

The heading stays at the top. In the live preview, lyric text starts at 200px and shrinks only as needed to fit the remaining space; the heading is always 1/φ of its size. The corner keeps one line: where you are on the left, the next entry after the arrow. On a blank or section break, the arrow and next entry highlight to show what Right will open. Space, the display-off key, is still a pure black.

Mouse controls · no visible zones

Try the corner controls, then move across the wall to feel the 40 / 20 / 40 click areas.

The new Service button opens a compact, scrollable entry list above the corner. Choose a numbered row to jump directly to its entry; the current row has a quiet marker. A slight overlay gives one consistent close cursor; clicking it closes the list without navigating. The bait numbers jump directly to that bait. Between baits, a blank rest keeps the song cue and highlights the next bait; after the last bait, it highlights the next entry instead. The arrow and next-entry text act as one button. On a passage, the translation label highlights on hover and flips TB / SIM on click, just like T; the new label briefly brightens. Above the 64px bottom band, the left 40% steps back, the right 40% steps forward, and the middle 20% hides or shows the words. A muted arrow or eye cursor hints at the action, then disappears after two seconds without movement. The corner cue and keyboard button fade together after 30 seconds without mouse or keyboard activity, then return on the next activity. They stay visible while the service list, Go To popup, or keyboard sheet is open; the Go To overlay catches outside clicks without dimming the words. If hidden deliberately with C, only C brings them back. Open the 1366 × 768 preview in this tab to inspect it at projector size.
